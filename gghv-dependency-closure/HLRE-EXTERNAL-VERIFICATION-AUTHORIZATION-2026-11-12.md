## 1. The authorization

The assistant has **pre-approved authorization** to open the following and run
the gate requirement through them itself:

- **Manus** (manus.ai)
- **Gemini 4 (Argon)** (Google AI Studio / Gemini)
- **Academia Co-Scientist** (Academia)

"Run the gate requirement through them" means: the source gate (a fresh pass
over primary material) and the balance gate (an independent check of key
quantitative identities) are run by the assistant through these three agents,
rather than by a human. This follows the §6.5 repeal, which removed the human
from the gate requirement; this amendment names what replaces the human.

## 2. The method

This is **the fully authorized method for every single thing that requires
outside verification**. Whenever a claim, computation, or assessment needs
verification from outside the assistant's own pipelines, the method is: the
assistant runs it through Manus, Gemini 4 (Argon), and/or Co-Scientist, as
the task fits, and carries the result through the gates. No other outside
verification method is needed, and no separate approval is needed to open
these three — the approval is given here, in advance, for all future
assessments.

## 3. The see-through requirement

The assistant is **required to see the entire interaction all the way
through** — from opening the agent and giving it the task, through its work,
to the result landing and being audited locally. No fire-and-forget launches;
no interaction left half-finished; no result counted before it arrives and is
checked.

## 4. Notification

During such a process the assistant notifies the user **only with detailed
status updates and results** — what was opened, what it is doing, what it
returned, what the local audit found, what it means for the grade. No
mid-process approval stops: the authorization is pre-approved, so the
interaction runs to completion and the user is kept informed in detail rather
than asked to approve steps along the way.
