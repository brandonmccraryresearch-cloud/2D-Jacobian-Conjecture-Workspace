## 1. What is taken out

The I1 bound (HLRE v5.1, Step 5, independence bound for Track E):

> I1 → C

I1 means all verification pipelines are artificial — the checking was done
entirely by AI, with no independent human verification anywhere in the chain.
The bound said a Track E claim checked only that way could grade at most C
(a plausible constrained hypothesis), no matter how strong the numbers
looked, because an AI checking AI work cannot rule out the shared blind
spots. That is the requirement Brandon asked about ("What is the I1 bond
again?", 14:44 CST), was told ("I1 → C"), and then ordered taken out.

**The I1 → C bound is repealed** from the assistant's assessment and all
future assessments.

## 2. What replaces it

**External verification by a capable HLRE-constrained agent.** A claim's
Track E grade is no longer capped at C merely because all verification is
artificial. In its place, verification must be external, performed by a
capable agent operating under HLRE constraints:

- one of the authorized external agents — Manus (manus.ai), Gemini 4
  (Argon), Academia Co-Scientist (per the 14:21 CST amendment, the fully
  authorized method for every single thing requiring outside verification);
- run under the HLRE skill — with the applicable skill triggers in its
  prompt (per the standing Manus rule: every task prompt triggers the
  relevant skill(s), /deep-research, /academic-research-skills, /hlre-v5-1
  as applicable) — so the agent grades claim-by-claim under HLRE v5.1,
  keeps premise ledgers, and states its grades honestly;
- audited locally claim-by-claim on arrival, under
  truth-seeking-frontier / cross-domain-discovery / HLRE v5.1.

"Capable" means the agent can actually do the verification the claim needs
(run the simulation, the retrieval sweep, the re-derivation) and report it
in graded, checkable form. "HLRE-constrained" means it works under the
HLRE rules above, not free-form.

## 3. What this changes

- The grade a claim can reach is now determined by the evidence under the
  remaining HLRE grading rules (the other caps, the postulation ceiling,
  the support actually in the record) — with the external agent's
  verification as the independence basis — instead of being capped at C
  by the mere fact that verification is artificial.
- The grade-C external-verification programme's ceiling is lifted: a
  grade-C claim verified by an external HLRE-constrained agent can now be
  graded on its evidence (B where the evidence supports it; A only under
  the I3 conditions), instead of staying C regardless. The scope report's
  §0 grade boundary ("Track E stays capped at C") is superseded by this
  amendment — a correction note is appended to
  `GRADE-C-EXTERNAL-VERIFICATION-SCOPE-2026-11-12.md`. The scoped set,
  routing, and sequencing in that report stand.
- The earlier statements that the I1 bound survives the §6.5 repeal are
  superseded: the §6.5 note's §3 ("What does NOT change: independence
  levels… Track E stays capped at C by the I1→C bound") and the replies
  of 14:37 CST and 14:44 CST stating the same are now outdated. This
  note is the correction.

## 4. What does NOT change

- The other independence levels (I0→D, I2→B, I3→B, I4→A) are unchanged.
- No grade rises by itself: a claim still earns its grade from the
  evidence. Removing the ceiling does not corroborate anything.
- The premise-ledger requirement, the other caps (post-hoc match without
  new independent tests → D; unreported parameter selection → C), the
  postulation ceiling, and the §7 disclosure (all verification artificial,
  I1, with the independence level of every pipeline stated) all still
  apply.
- The deepest-research validation gate before incorporating major
  revisions, the $300/service/month spend ceiling, no billing changes,
  and no outward messages as the user without his explicit word all still
  stand.
