\[
T(a_4)=9a_4^{10}+37200a_4^5+95051008.

## Back-substitution

The three linear Gröbner-basis relations
\[
21a_4^3-72a_3a_4+91a_2=0,
\]
\[
12a_3a_4^2-99a_2a_4+416a_1=0,
\]
\[
a_2a_4^2-10a_1a_4+182=0

\[
30408a_4^3.
\]
Because \(T(0)=95051008\neq0\), every root of \(T\) has \(a_4\neq0\), so the system is invertible on the eliminant locus.

The resulting branch parametrization is

\boxed{a_1=\frac{3a_4^5+13078}{362a_4}},

Thus every algebraic root \(a_4\) of \(T\) determines one corresponding quadruple \((a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4)\) on the reduced B2.6 system.
