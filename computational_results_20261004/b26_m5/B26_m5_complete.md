The recurrence is, for $e = (3m-1)/2 = 7$:
$$
B_k = -\frac{1}{1+2k}\sum_{i=1}^{5}\bigl(1+2(k-i)-3i\bigr)A_iB_{k-i},
\qquad k = 1,\dots,7,
$$
with $B_0 = 1$, $A = (1, a_1, a_2, a_3, a_4, 1)$.

The residual equations (for $n = 8, 9, 10, 11$) are:
$$

$$
where $B_j = 0$ for $j > 7$ or $j < 0$. The nominal fifth residual ($n=12$)
is identically zero.
