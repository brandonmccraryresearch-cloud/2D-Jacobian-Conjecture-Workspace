### Main Theorem (§8, Thm 1.1)
- **Lean**: `main_theorem_of_chart` in `Jacobian/BranchAbChart.lean` — states the **NewtonNF2 form** (no P,Q satisfy NewtonNF2 with [P,Q]=λx², λ≠0), conditional on ChartClassification; **v17**: `main_theorem` in `Jacobian/ChartProof/Final.lean` = `main_theorem_of_chart (chartClassification_holds L)`, **unconditional**
- **Paper listings**: `lst:chart` (`main_theorem_of_chart`); v17: `lst:unconditional` (`main_theorem`)
- **Statement**: If P,Q satisfy the weak hypothesis (support in N_P,N_Q; a_{1,0}a_{8,14}b_{2,1}b_{12,21}≠0; [P,Q]=λx², λ≠0), then b₁₂,₂₄=0 and a₈,₁₆=0. In particular, no P,Q satisfy NewtonNF2 with [P,Q]=λx², λ≠0.
- **Logical status**: The b₁₂,₂₄=0 part follows from `no_completion_K5_PQ` + `topLayerClassification_of_chart` + `chartClassification_holds` (v17); the a₈,₁₆=0 part is computer algebra (`a816_full.sing`). The NewtonNF2 form is one unconditional kernel-checked theorem (`main_theorem`).
